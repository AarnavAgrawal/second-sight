One call at a time, state recorded: no two updates ever collide.

Every plane we’re watching is its own Terse Durable Actor. The NORTH agent and the radar feed both talk to it, but it handles one call at a time, so a new clearance and a position update can never collide. It records the plane’s current state, altitude, speed, clearance and status, and sets its own timer to re-check conformance. The state survives restarts.
